# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers should make participation respectful, specific and safe. Discuss the work without attacking the person. Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes, protect private information and give people a reasonable chance to clarify intent.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, deliberately disruptive conduct and retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where someone represents ScanFit. Technical disagreement is expected; personal hostility is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through the contact options on [Ahmedsultan09's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Ahmedsultan09). Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately as the available channel permits, without a guaranteed response time.
